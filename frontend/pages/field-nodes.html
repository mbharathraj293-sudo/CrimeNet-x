ESP32 Field Node Telemetry Specifications
Communication Protocol

Edge hardware communicates via 802.11 b/g/n Wi-Fi sending HTTP/REST payloads (JSON) to /api/events and /api/nodes/{id}/status.

Hardware Simulation

Physical ESP32 microcontrollers or the companion software simulator in esp32/ dispatch simulated sensor events and periodic battery telemetry.

Academic Ethical Scope

Sensors record synthetic environmental and spatial signals only. No real biometric, facial, or real-world surveillance hardware is permitted.
