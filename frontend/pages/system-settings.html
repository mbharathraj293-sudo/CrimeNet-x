Academic Ethical Safeguards Matrix
Strict Synthetic Guarantee

All records within CRIMENET-X are generated through pseudo-random spatial and statistical algorithms. No personally identifiable real-world human data exists in this environment.

No Automated Judicial Decisions

The platform contains zero automated enforcement logic. High-scoring pattern clusters require human analyst validation and cannot trigger any physical or legal action.

Prohibited Biometric Systems

Facial recognition, voice biometrics, gait identification, and real-world video surveillance pipelines are strictly prohibited and disabled in this architecture.

Pure Statistical Terminology

All metrics are designated as Analytical Pattern Scores or Spatial Recurrence Rates. No terminology implying criminal guilt or probability of future crime is permitted.
